## find-and-move-animal
Given a task to find and move an animal to a specific location, this workflow identifies the animal and moves it to the target location.
Step 1: explore environment to locate {animal}
Step 2: focus on {animal}
Step 3: teleport to {target-container-location}
Step 4: look around to locate {target-container}
Step 5: pick up {animal}
Step 6: move {animal} to {target-container}
