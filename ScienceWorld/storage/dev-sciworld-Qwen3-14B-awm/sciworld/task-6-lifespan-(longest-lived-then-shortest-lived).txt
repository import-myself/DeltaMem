## longest-lived-then-shortest-lived
Given a set of animals in a location, this workflow identifies the animal with the longest life span, then the animal with the shortest life span.
Step 1: teleport to {location}
Step 2: look around
Step 3: identify {animals}
Step 4: examine {animal}
Step 5: examine {animal}
Step 6: examine {animal}
Step 7: focus on {animal-with-longest-lifespan}
Step 8: focus on {animal-with-shortest-lifespan}
