## fruit-salad-creation
Given a kitchen environment with a recipe and ingredients, this workflow creates fruit salad by gathering and mixing the required fruits.
Step 1: teleport to {location}
Step 2: look around
Step 3: examine {object-name}
Step 4: read {object-name}
Step 5: pick up {object-name} from {container}
Step 6: teleport to {location}
Step 7: pick up {object-name}
Step 8: teleport to {location}
Step 9: pour {substance} from {container} into {container}
Step 10: teleport to {location}
Step 11: pick up {object-name}
Step 12: pour {object-name} into {container}
Step 13: move {object-name} to {container}
Step 14: mix {container}
