## find-and-move-non-living-object
Given a task to find a non-living thing and move it to a specific location, this workflow identifies a suitable non-living object and transports it to the target container.
Step 1: teleport to {location}
Step 2: look around
Step 3: focus on {object-name}
Step 4: teleport to {target-location}
Step 5: look around
Step 6: pick up {object-name}
Step 7: move {object-name} to {target-container}
Step 8: if {object-name} not found in {target-location}, teleport to {alternative-location}
Step 9: look around
Step 10: focus on {object-name}
Step 11: teleport to {target-location}
Step 12: pick up {object-name}
Step 13: move {object-name} to {target-container}
