## test-conductivity-workflow
Given a substance and a set of electrical components, this workflow determines if the substance is electrically conductive by creating a simple circuit and testing the substance's effect on the circuit.
Step 1: {teleport to workshop}
Step 2: {look around}
Step 3: {focus on substance}
Step 4: {examine battery}
Step 5: {examine wire}
Step 6: {connect battery anode to wire terminal 1}
Step 7: {examine light bulb}
Step 8: {connect wire terminal 2 to light bulb anode}
Step 9: {activate switch}
Step 10: {move substance to purple box}
