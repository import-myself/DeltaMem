## find-plant-and-move-to-box
Given a task to find a plant and move it to a specific container in another room, this workflow locates the plant and transfers it to the target container.
Step 1: teleport to {location} containing {plant}
Step 2: look around
Step 3: focus on {plant}
Step 4: pick up {plant}
Step 5: teleport to {target-room}
Step 6: look around
Step 7: move {plant} to {target-container}
