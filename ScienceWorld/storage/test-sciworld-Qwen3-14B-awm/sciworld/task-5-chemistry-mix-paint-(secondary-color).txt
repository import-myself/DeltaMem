## mix-secondary-color
Given two primary paint colors, this workflow creates a secondary color by mixing them.
Step 1: teleport to {location}
Step 2: look around
Step 3: open {cupboard}
Step 4: pour {color1} into an empty container
Step 5: pour {color2} into the container with {color1}
Step 6: mix {container}
Step 7: focus on {resulting-color}
