## lifespan-comparison-workflow
Given a set of animals in a location, this workflow identifies the animal with the longest life span and then the one with the shortest life span.
Step 1: teleport to {location}
Step 2: look around
Step 3: examine {animal}
Step 4: examine {animal}
Step 5: examine {animal}
Step 6: focus on {longest-lived-animal}
Step 7: focus on {shortest-lived-animal}
