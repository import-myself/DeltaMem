## Flight Check-In
Given a confirmation number and last name, this workflow checks in for a flight and includes steps to access the baggage fee calculator.
[tab] Check-in tab -> ACTION: {confirmation-number}
[textbox] Confirmation or ticket number* -> ACTION: {confirmation-number}
[textbox] Last name* -> ACTION: {last-name}
[button] Search -> ACTION: {}
[tab] TRAVEL INFO -> ACTION: {}
[link] Baggage info -> ACTION: CLICK [4547]
[link] Baggage fee calculator -> ACTION: CLICK [5555]
